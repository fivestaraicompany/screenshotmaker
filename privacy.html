Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Screenshot (App) Maker ("the App") handles user data.

No accounts. No analytics. No tracking.
Your files and edits stay on your device unless you choose to connect to your own Ollama server.
If you use Ollama, text is sent only to the server address you configure.
01

Information Collection

  • The App does not collect, store, or transmit personal information to our servers.
  • Screenshots, templates, and exported images are created and saved locally on your device.
02

Files and Photos Access

  • The App may request access to the Files app or your photo library to import screenshots.
  • Imported images are processed on-device and are not uploaded automatically.
03

AI Translation (Ollama)

  • If you enable AI text generation or translation, the App can send text to an Ollama server that you configure (for example, your own computer).
  • We do not operate or receive your Ollama traffic, and the App does not log or store the text processed by AI.
04

Third-Party Services

  • The App does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies.
05

Changes

  • We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page.
06

Contact

  • If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the email address provided on the App Store listing.
